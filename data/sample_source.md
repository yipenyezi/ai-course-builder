# Sample source: Human-in-the-loop AI review

AI can accelerate repetitive knowledge-work tasks, but high-quality outputs still require human judgment. A human-in-the-loop workflow keeps a person responsible for reviewing, editing, and approving AI-generated content before it is used in a consequential setting.

A strong review process should define what the AI can draft, what a human must verify, what quality criteria apply, and how errors are documented. The goal is not simply to maximize automation. The goal is to use automation where it creates value while preserving accountability, accuracy, and appropriate judgment.

For learning-content workflows, reviewers may check factual accuracy, alignment to learning objectives, terminology consistency, tone, accessibility, and whether the generated assessment actually measures the intended skill.

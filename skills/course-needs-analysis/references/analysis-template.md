# Needs analysis: guided inputs and decision brief

Use existing material first. The ID may complete this template directly or supply documents for AI-assisted extraction. Separate source-supported facts, stakeholder reports, hypotheses, and proposed synthetic details. Ask focused questions about the highest-impact gaps. This is a template, not a demand for every field on every project; record why a lens is not applicable rather than fabricating an answer.

## 2. Five analysis lenses

| Lens | Guided questions | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Organization | What result matters and why now? Who owns it? Are expectations and priorities consistent? | Goal, measures, constraints, stakeholder decision |
| Gap | What should happen and what actually happens? In which tasks/conditions? Is this a new requirement or an observed shortfall? | Comparable expected/actual performance and evidence limitations |
| Learners | What do performers already know/do? Which groups differ? What language, accessibility, technology support, authority, and time do they need? | Shared learner brief with evidence, design implications, unknowns |
| Tasks | What triggers the task, which actions/decisions matter, and what defines good work? How frequent, difficult, and consequential are errors? | Initial task map and priorities; detailed SME follow-up |
| Environment | Which tools, information, resources, incentives, permissions, handoffs, and manager support affect performance? Where will learning and application happen? | Work barriers and learning/technical constraints |

Learner brief fields: role/group, task scope, relevant prior knowledge, prerequisite evidence, language/literacy demands, access needs, digital familiarity, work schedule, device/screen, connectivity/audio availability, allowed job aids, and implications for scaffolding. Collect only relevant information; do not infer needs from demographic categories. Record delivery preferences separately from feasible/effective delivery requirements.

Technical fields: tool/host, supported devices/browsers, media and embedding constraints, LMS/LRS/identity availability, online/offline needs, data access/storage permissions, and IT verification owner. Availability is not assumed merely because employees use computers at work.

## Synthetic decision contrast

| Situation | Appropriate conclusion |
|---|---|
| Sellers explain discovery correctly but the workflow prevents access to customer records; observed delays align with that barrier | Address access/workflow; no established knowledge/skill need from those facts alone |
| Observations reveal unsupported recommendations and a practice sample shows difficulty eliciting needs | Investigate/confirm a discovery skill gap and consider targeted practice, not automatically a broad platform course |
| Both patterns occur | Combined intervention, with separate owners and measures |
| One fictional call is the only evidence | Insufficient evidence for a population diagnosis; collect focused additional evidence |

Original project template based on the established gap → workflow → content-selection approach. Use public or synthetic examples in this repository. Do not copy transcripts or confidential operational details into published artifacts.

## Interview prompts

| Focus | Primary question | Useful follow-up |
|---|---|---|
| Work trigger/output | What starts this task, and what does successful completion look like? | What evidence would show it is complete? What are the inputs, tools, permissions, and handoffs? |
| Incident reconstruction | Walk through a specific instance from beginning to end. | At that moment, what could the performer see or know? What happened next? |
| Expert cues | Where did you need to judge rather than simply follow steps? | What did you notice? What would a novice miss? Could you demonstrate your reasoning? |
| Alternatives | Which actions seemed possible at that point? | Under what conditions would another action be valid? |
| Mistakes | What errors actually occur, and why are they tempting? | What observation supports that? Is this widespread, local, or a single incident? |
| Exceptions/recovery | When does the normal path not apply? | When should someone stop, look up guidance, recover, or escalate? Who has authority? |
| Consequences | What changes after each action? | Is the outcome inevitable, conditional, or only possible? What source confirms it? |
| Information support | What must be available from memory versus looked up? | Could a checklist or decision table support it? Can people access it during work? |
| Priority/feasibility | How frequent, difficult, and consequential is this task? | What practice is feasible for these learners? What environmental changes are still needed? |
| Validation | What have we misunderstood or omitted? | Which claims require another owner, source, or performer perspective? |

## Review and handoff

- SME: factual accuracy, valid alternatives, exceptions, consequences, source authority.
- ID: course scope, classifications, prerequisite fit, time feasibility, job aids, candidate practice.
- Sponsor/process owner: unresolved intervention dependencies or policy decisions.
- Record version, feedback, disposition, reviewer and date; pending remains pending.
- Transfer selected content and task/source IDs to objectives/course-map design; retain unresolved risks and excluded items for traceability.

## Synthetic example

Gap G1 concerns discovery before recommendation. Note N1 describes a fictional seller recommending a migration before clarifying the customer's business outcome. The mistake is a scenario proposal, not a claim about a real sales population.

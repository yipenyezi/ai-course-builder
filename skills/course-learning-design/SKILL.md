# Workplace learning design

Use the approved project evidence and `docs/course-design-template.md`. Read [principles](references/principles.md) when choosing practice or reviewing learning claims. These instructions guide design; the app does not yet load skills automatically.

Use the [course map and strategy template](references/course-map-template.md) to translate analysis into a coherent sequence. Strategy is derived from evidence and constraints, not selected independently from a menu of attractive interactions.

Read [learning objective guidance](references/learning-objectives.md) before writing or revising LOs. Derive terminal work behavior and necessary enabling objectives; use Bloom to describe actual task demands, not a verb lookup. Add meaningful conditions/criteria without manufacturing numerical targets or outcomes outside the learner's control. Keep formal objectives distinct from concise learner-facing purpose statements.

## Decisions

For an unresolved training request or incomplete performance diagnosis, first use `skills/course-needs-analysis/SKILL.md`. Its approved evidence, learner brief, and intervention decision are inputs here; do not treat a request for content as a confirmed training need.

1. Establish the business result, observable work actions, and barriers. Distinguish supported causes from hypotheses. Stop course production when evidence does not support training; recommend investigation or non-training intervention.
2. Use SME incidents, decision cues, mistakes, and exceptions to draft realistic practice. Select minimum supporting information and job aids; preserve a reasoned cut list. Finalize feasible SMART objectives after workflow and scope review.
3. Build a course map linking gap → action → objective → demonstration/practice → assessment. Include learner prerequisites, available time, and approved resources. Choose a guided example when novices lack a usable mental model; do not make every lesson an unsupported discovery exercise.
4. Select retrieval, spacing, varied/interleaved practice, generation, and reflection when they serve the task. State what the learner actually does and how the design elicits it. Do not add all techniques mechanically or claim that engagement establishes retention.
5. Early sales practice may offer plausible fixed choices. Later AI role-play must be free-form with no suggested replies. Keep private assessment separate from the counterpart; hints appear only on request, formal personalized feedback at the end.
6. Elicit an attempt before revealing solutions where appropriate. Every authored choice needs reasoning and a plausible consequence. Confirm assessment aspect/count and feedback timing with the ID. Check answer-length and position clues across the item set.

## Output and checks

Before finalizing the design artifact, verify approved training/combined diagnosis, confirmed SME workflow/content scope, shared learner brief, and SMART objective decisions. Conditional sketches may surface missing decisions, but must not be labeled approved. Record source IDs and rationale for delivery format, scaffolding, sequence, practice, and assessment choices. If new evidence contradicts the diagnosis or scope, return the issue upstream instead of silently redesigning around it.

Deliver a concise decision table: objective/task, learner action, practice strategy and rationale, feedback timing, supporting information/job aid, evidence elicited, transfer/follow-up, and unresolved ID/SME decisions. Link existing artifact IDs rather than regenerating approved content silently.

Evaluate using Kirkpatrick planning from results backward; simulated performance is Level 2, not proof of Level 3 transfer. Include delayed or workplace checks where feasible and mark unavailable data. Do not invent mastery thresholds, schedules, or business impact. Route visual implementation to `skills/course-visual-design/SKILL.md` only after instructional decisions are clear.

For detailed item writing, blueprint confirmation, answer rationales, and set-level review, use `skills/course-assessment-design/SKILL.md`. Keep this skill focused on the course strategy rather than duplicating item-writing rules.
